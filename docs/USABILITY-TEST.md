# Playtest protocol

## Invitation draft

I'm testing CS2 AI Coach, a demo-review tool that shows opening deaths and the
events behind its review notes. Would you spend about 15 minutes trying it and
telling me what is clear or confusing? You can use the anonymized sample and
keep your account details and demos private. It is fine to stop at any point.

Sample: https://cs2-ai-coach-demo.vercel.app

## Session

1. Ask permission to take anonymous notes. Explain how feedback will be used.
   Recording is optional and requires separate consent.
2. Ask the tester to open the sample, choose a player, and locate an opening
   death. Record completion and the first point of confusion.
3. Ask them to explain what the tool verified and what remains uncertain.
4. Ask which round they would review and what they would watch for.
5. Ask what would make the tool worth using on their own match.

Use participant IDs P01 through P05. Record consent, completion, blockers,
suggested replay action, and one optional direct quote only with permission.
Prioritize blockers by frequency and severity, then address or ticket the
top two. Retain anonymized notes and avoid account IDs or private demo files.

Sessions completed: 0/5. No recruitment messages have been sent.
