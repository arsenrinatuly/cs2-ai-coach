# Product requirements

The MVP helps a CS2 player review a recorded match. A player uploads a Source 2
demo, sees processing status, selects a recorded player, and reviews statistics
and evidence-linked coaching suggestions.

## Scope

- Validate and process a local CS2 demo and provide actionable input errors.
- Present recorded map, rounds, player identities, kills, deaths, assists,
  participation, and opening deaths where the data supports them.
- Link factual coaching observations to match, round, and event evidence.
  Distinguish measurements, interpretations, and suggested actions.
- Exercise one complete demo-to-report workflow and record evaluation results.

## Out of scope

Live analysis, video analysis, FACEIT account integration, payments,
multi-agent coaching, and claims about player intentions are outside the MVP.
